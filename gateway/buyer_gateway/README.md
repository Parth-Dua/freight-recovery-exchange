# Hormuz Exchange

Ship cargo by chatting: say **"I need to ship cargo"** and fill in the card.

- Independent carrier agents (Atlas, Beacon, Cove, Meridian, Dhow) quote with their own private pricing; your buyer agent negotiates for you without revealing your budget.
- Pick an offer from the cards, confirm, and pay by card through the Fetch Payment Protocol (Stripe checkout).
- If a carrier withdraws your sailing, the exchange negotiates replacements with the other carriers and rebooks every affected shipment together, within the extra cost you allowed, or asks you first.
