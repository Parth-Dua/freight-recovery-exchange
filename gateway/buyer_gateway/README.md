# Hormuz Exchange: Ocean Freight Booking & Container Shipping Agent

Book container shipping in chat. Tell the agent what cargo you need to move; it gets quotes from independent
ocean carriers, negotiates the price for you, books the sailing, takes card payment, and automatically
rebooks your cargo if a carrier cancels the sailing.

## What it does
- **Container shipping quotes:** 20-foot containers (20GP) on Gulf and Indian Ocean lanes: Jebel Ali (Dubai),
  Sohar, Dammam, Mumbai, Mundra, Karachi.
- **Freight rate negotiation:** your buyer agent negotiates with every eligible carrier in parallel (up to two
  counteroffers each) without revealing your budget, then shows the best offers.
- **Negotiation transcripts:** see exactly how each carrier's price moved.
- **Booking and payment:** the carrier holds the space, you pay by card (Stripe), the carrier confirms and
  you get a booking receipt.
- **Disruption recovery:** if your sailing is cancelled (security incident, port congestion, vessel fault),
  the exchange negotiates replacement space and rebooks you within the extra cost you allowed, or asks first.

## Try asking
- "I need to ship cargo"
- "Ship 2 containers from Jebel Ali to Mumbai by next week"
- "Get me freight quotes for a 20ft container from Dubai to India"
- "Book ocean freight from Dammam to Mumbai"
- "My sailing was cancelled, rebook my container"

## How to use
1. Say what you want to ship; a form card asks for route, containers and deadline.
2. Pick one of the negotiated offers from the cards.
3. Confirm and pay by card; your booking confirmation arrives in the chat.
